3 Next they tried the hallway. A pale circle rested on the rug. Alice knelt beside it, and Bob curled his tail around her wrist. The circle came from a small lamp behind them. Grandma brought a blanket and joined them in the mild evening air.

4 Beyond the garden fence, the moon rose above the apple tree. Its light lay along the leaves like a thin silver ribbon. Alice placed the cushion beside her, but Bob chose her lap instead. 5 Alice looked up at the moon and counted the little lights beside it. Bob watched a leaf turn slowly on its stem.
